Radar Mono is a subset of Cilantro Code Mono (Regular and Bold), reduced to
Latin, punctuation, arrows and box/geometric symbols. Because it is a modified
version, it is renamed and does not use the Reserved Font Names
"Cilantro Code" or "香菜碼".
